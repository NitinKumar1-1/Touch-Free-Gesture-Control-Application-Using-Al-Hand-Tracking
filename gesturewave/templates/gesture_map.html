📌 Usage Tips

  • Ensure your hand is clearly visible in good lighting
  • Keep your hand 30–80 cm from the webcam for best accuracy
  • Hold gestures for ~0.5 seconds for reliable detection
  • Use a plain background for improved detection performance
  • Only one hand is tracked at a time; use your dominant hand
